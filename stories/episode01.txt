TITLE:
The Lion and the Little Rabbit

LANGUAGE:
Hindi + English

MORAL:
Never underestimate someone because of their size.

CHARACTERS:
Leo - a proud but kind lion
Mimi - a clever little rabbit
Tara - an old wise tortoise
Other forest animals

STORY:
Create an original 8-minute cartoon animal moral story.
The story should be suitable for children.
Use simple Hindi and simple English.
Keep the story funny, emotional and adventurous.
Do not copy any existing story, movie or YouTube video.

The story should contain:
1. Strong opening hook
2. Introduction of characters
3. A problem in the forest
4. Adventure and funny moments
5. A clever solution
6. Emotional ending
7. Clear moral lesson

Write narration and dialogues separately.
